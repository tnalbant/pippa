# Pippa 0.4.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.4.0**

- **A new AI on your Mac.** Pippa now uses one AI, Ornith 1.5 9B, built for Apple silicon. It is made for Apple's chips and writes clean German. If oMLX, LM Studio or Hugging Face already has it on your Mac, Pippa uses that copy instead of downloading it again. Macs with less than 16 GB of memory use an online AI instead.
- **A new conversation window.** Conversations in the sidebar show what they were about and the start of the answer; you can pin and rename them. While Pippa works, one line says what she is doing right now; Details shows every step.
- **See what she read and did.** A side panel shows the files and pages she read, what she added on your Mac, which dates she checked, and how full the conversation is. Files she read appear under the answer; click one to open it.
- **Dates she worked out are marked.** If an answer names a date that isn't in anything she read, for example "four weeks after receipt" turned into a date, it is highlighted with a note to check it.
- **Clearer when something is too much.** If a question is too big for the AI on your Mac, Pippa says so instead of stopping without an answer.
- **Fewer permissions.** Pippa no longer asks for Contacts or the microphone.

**Good to know:** setup downloads the new AI once, about 5.4 GB. Once it is ready, the old AI files from earlier versions go to the Trash, which frees several GB.
