# Pippa 0.3.20

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 17 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.20**

- **Pippa works in your Mac apps.** She can look into Mail folders, Notes, Contacts, Finder, Safari and Numbers through AppleScript. Looking happens right away; before she changes anything (moving a mail, creating a note) she asks you. She never sends mail and never empties the Trash.
- **Pippa reads attachments.** When you hand her an email from Mail, or she opens one, she saves its attachments (PDFs, documents, pictures) as files and can read and explain them. Signature logos are left out.
- **She can open an email she found.** After a search she reads the whole email and its attachments instead of only its first lines.
- **Less noise in her instructions.** Notices such as "Pippa was updated" no longer travel with every question as if they were part of the task.
- **A calmer conversation window.** The message box is a soft raised surface, Send and Stop share one button in the same place, steps show a small spinner while they run and a check when done, and the jump-down button says "New Answer" when one arrives out of view.
